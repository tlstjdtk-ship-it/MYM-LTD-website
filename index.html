RESEARCH PROCESS

A repeatable decision framework

  1. Identify the market regime using index trends, volatility and liquidity.
  2. Compare leading and lagging sectors using relative strength and volume.
  3. Evaluate business fundamentals, earnings growth and price behaviour.
  4. Apply predefined risk limits and review outcomes without promising returns.
